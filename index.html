About

How CostumeCraft was built with Kiro

CostumeCraft is a static planner. You choose a party, a cosplay, a kids' event, or the office, add up to three vibes, set a budget and a skill level, and the page writes a concept name, a character brief, a priced materials list, DIY steps, and three alternatives.

The generator is a rule and template engine in js/engine.js. It does not call a server. If you want a model to rewrite the prose, you can add your own key. The shopping list does not change when that happens.

Kiro's project files are in /.kiro. Steering docs keep the offline plan, the kids and office safety lines, and this dark UI from drifting. The spec in .kiro/specs/costume-planner is the product contract: requirements, design, and tasks. A hook on js/engine.js asks an agent to confirm every plan still has a name, a brief, materials, steps, a cost, and alternatives.

The live demo is yuu2001.github.io/costume-craft-kiroween. The Pages workflow publishes this site from main. Last plans are saved with localStorage. Use Print when you want a paper checklist.
